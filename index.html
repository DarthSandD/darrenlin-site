Flagship · Building Services

MEP Suits

Building Services Mechanical Toolkit — built by an engineer, for the plant room. Eight calculators: Duct Sizer, Psychrometrics, Ventilation (ASHRAE 62.1), Pipe Hydraulics, Water & Coil Loads, Fan Laws, Room Cooling Load, and Unit Converter — plus a 3D System Viewer (Outdoor AC unit, duct runs, industrial pipes) live-linked to Duct + Pipe.

Every tool shows its method / formula and checks against ASHRAE / CIBSE design bands. Design aid — results must be verified by a licensed engineer before construction / procurement.

Architecture

Why three flagships prove the architecture.

VoltWorks and MEP Suits are systems: orchestration to build them, verification loops to check them, and coherent designs that hold calculators, 3D, and diagrams together. Patterns is the other half: the judgment behind choosing the right system in the first place.
